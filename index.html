                  How we read candidates

                  • Launch experience beats a big-company title. For CEO or GM roles, look for someone who has started something close to the top. A single big-company career needs real proof, such as running a subsidiary or overseas unit.
                  • Write the requirement before the search. If founders and investors disagree on the role, interviews will not fix it.
                  • Deploy AI talent in pairs. A domain expert plus an AI engineer usually gets further than searching for one person who does both.
